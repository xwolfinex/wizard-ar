Wizard AR Test

This is a quick, free prototype built on AR.js (no app, no platform subscription). Point your phone's camera at the marker image below and your wizard GIF should appear floating over it.

This is the "Hiro" marker AR.js ships with. Print it, or pull it up on a second phone/screen and point this page's camera at that screen.
Needs camera permission and works best in good light. This is a stand-in test marker, for the real show you'd swap this for a trained custom marker (the mascot artwork itself, or a QR-adjacent graphic) once the look is locked.
